    How to play

    Each day brings a new region, cut into squares. A square's colour shows the local majority; paler squares are more mixed, and bigger dots mean more people.

    Drag along the grid lines to draw a border. A border only counts once it closes off a territory, running edge to edge or into another line. Loose ends show dashed. Tap a line to remove it, or drag back over it.

    Make two territories (three on some maps), then submit. In each territory the largest group is the majority, and everyone else is on the wrong side. Lower is better.

    You'll then see the best border we could find. It is almost never zero: people are too mixed for any line to separate them cleanly.

    Maps are simplified models built from approximate census figures, counting only the largest groups. "About this map", under the map, gives each map's sources and history.
